# Hook Library — 前三行公式庫

## 1. The Three-Line Mechanic

Each line has a job. Violating this wastes the only real estate that matters.

| Line | Job |
|---|---|
| 1 | **製造缺口** — open a question the reader now needs closed |
| 2 | **加壓或給具體** — raise the stakes, or hand over a number/detail that makes it credible |
| 3 | **停在懸崖邊** — end at maximum tension. **Never resolve here.** |

The most common failure is answering your own hook on line 3. If the reader learns the conclusion before the fold, there is no reason to tap.

**Hard rules:**
- Write the hook **last**, after the body exists — a hook can only advertise fuel that's actually in there.
- Line 1 stands alone. If it needs line 2 to make sense, it isn't a line 1.
- Short lines. A "line" on a phone is ~20 全形字; a long sentence silently eats two.
- Numbers on line 1 or 2 whenever the fuel allows.

## 2. The Seven Formulas

### 公式一｜反直覺結論 (Counter-Intuitive Result)
**Bets on:** cognitive dissonance. A held belief is contradicted; the tap is how the reader resolves it.
**Needs:** a real outcome that genuinely contradicts common sense.

- 「我把公司的 FB 廣告全砍了，業績反而漲了 40%。」
- 「我們把價格調高一倍，客訴反而變少了。」
- 「練了三年，我終於承認：跑步減不了肥。」

⚠️ The reversal must be real. Manufactured contrarianism reads as bait and the body can't pay it off.

### 公式二｜具體數字＋時間框 (Number × Timeframe)
**Bets on:** specificity = credibility. Odd numbers outperform round ones — 17 beats 20, 23 萬 beats 20 萬 — because round numbers read as estimates and odd ones read as counted.
**Needs:** real numbers. Never invent these.

- 「30 天、17 支影片、0 元廣告費，我學到 3 件事。」
- 「我花了 8 個月才想通的事，你 3 分鐘就能看完。」
- 「連續 90 天早上 5 點起床之後，我後悔了。」

### 公式三｜身分標籤點名 (Identity Call-Out)
**Bets on:** 對號入座. Counter-intuitively, **narrowing the audience widens the reach** — a precisely named group reacts hard, and hard reactions are what get a post promoted into the recommendation pool.
**Needs:** a specific, self-recognizable identity. 「給所有想成長的人」 names nobody.

- 「如果你是接案設計師，這篇可能會讓你不太舒服。」
- 「給那些做了三年、還在接 5000 塊案子的人。」
- 「35 歲以後才想轉職的，這篇是寫給你的。」

### 公式四｜中途切入的場景 (Mid-Scene Cut)
**Bets on:** narrative gap. Start at peak tension, skip all setup. The reader taps to find out what happened.
**Needs:** a real first-hand moment.

- 「昨天客戶傳來一句話，我盯著螢幕看了五分鐘。」
- 「面試官看完我的作品集，只說了三個字。」
- 「凌晨兩點，合夥人打來說他要退出。」

Strongest of the seven for dwell time — narrative pulls readers deeper than argument does.

### 公式五｜代價揭露 (Cost Confession)
**Bets on:** loss aversion. Attention paid to *someone else's pit* far exceeds attention paid to their success. Also the highest-trust formula — admitting cost buys credibility nothing else can.
**Needs:** a real failure and its real price.

- 「這個決定讓我賠掉 23 萬，我把過程完整寫下來。」
- 「我用三年時間證明了一件事：這條路走不通。」
- 「我最貴的一堂課，學費是一個離職的員工。」

### 公式六｜敵人指認 (Name the Enemy)
**Bets on:** attribution transfer. It re-attributes the reader's failure from 「我不夠好」 to an external, named enemy. This produces the strongest 加註轉發 rate of any formula — the reader shares to declare a position.
**Needs:** a defensible enemy — a practice, a myth, a default. **Never a demographic or a named person.**

- 「真正殺死你影片流量的不是演算法，是第 3 秒。」
- 「你不是不會行銷，你只是被『先做好產品』這句話騙了。」
- 「讓你加班的不是工作量，是那場每週三的會議。」

Pattern: 「不是 A，是 B」 — where A is what they blame and B is your actual insight.

### 公式七｜禁忌洩漏 (Insider Leak)
**Bets on:** scarcity = value. Implies information that wasn't supposed to leave the room.
**Needs:** genuinely non-public, genuinely useful material.

- 「這件事同業大概不會希望我講。」
- 「接案十年，我從來沒公開講過報價是怎麼算的。今天講。」

⚠️ Highest bait-risk of the seven. If the body doesn't deliver something actually insider, this is pure 標題黨 — it spikes bounce and taxes every subsequent post. Use only when the goods are real.

## 3. Anti-Patterns

Each of these is a reach killer. Scan the draft for all of them.

| Anti-pattern | Example | Why it dies |
|---|---|---|
| **暖場句** | 「大家好，今天想跟大家分享一下…」 | Spends the entire hook saying nothing. **Fix: delete downward until you hit a real sentence — that's your actual line 1.** |
| **自我介紹** | 「我是 XXX，一個做了五年的…」 | Nobody cares who you are until you've earned it. Credentials go in the body, after value. |
| **弱疑問句** | 「不知道大家有沒有這種經驗？」 | The reader's honest answer is 「沒有」, and then they scroll. |
| **純情緒感嘆** | 「太震撼了！」「必看！」 | Emotion is the output you want, not an input you can declare. |
| **摘要式開頭** | 「這篇要跟大家聊聊 AI 影片的五個重點」 | You just gave away the whole post. Nothing left to tap for. |
| **兌現不了的標題黨** | 「99% 的人都不知道…」 | The ranker watches bounce; unpaid hooks damage every future post, not just this one. |
| **形容詞堆疊** | 「超級實用又完整的懶人包」 | Your conclusion, not their evidence. |
| **在第 3 行給答案** | — | The single most common technical failure. Ends tension instead of holding it. |

## 4. Selection & Sharpening

**Generate at least 6 across different formulas, ship 4** (1 main + 3 alternates). The three alternates must each use a **different formula** — they exist to test different reader psychologies, not different wordings of one idea.

**Sharpening passes:**
1. **刪到不能再刪** — cut every word that isn't load-bearing. Hooks die of fat.
2. **具體化** — 「很多人」→「做了三年的接案設計師」; 「效果很好」→「40%」.
3. **前置衝擊** — move the most surprising word as early in line 1 as grammar allows.
4. **截斷測試** — read *only* the 3 lines, as a stranger with no context. Tap or scroll? Be honest; this is the whole game.

**Matching formula to 戰略軸:**

| 戰略軸 | Prefer | Because |
|---|---|---|
| 分享型 | 敵人指認、反直覺結論 | They hand the reader a position to declare — the engine of 加註轉發 |
| 追蹤型 | 代價揭露、中途切入、數字×時間框 | They demonstrate ongoing production and first-hand depth — the engine of 追蹤轉換 |
| 轉單型 | 身分標籤點名 | Precision beats breadth; you want the right 200 people, not the wrong 20,000 |
