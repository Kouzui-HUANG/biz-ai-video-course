## 1. Poster Types (choose the campaign role)

| Type | Campaign role | Content rule | Risk if misused |
|---|---|---|---|
| **概念先導 Teaser** | First reveal, months out | ONE motif or silhouette, heavy negative space, no faces required | Too cryptic reads as "no film here" |
| **主視覺 Theatrical One-Sheet** | The workhorse; cinema lobby, streaming tile | Protagonist inside the world, full depth layers, central tension visible | Becomes a floating-head collage |
| **角色海報 Character Poster** | Series of 3–6, one per lead | One character, identical framing & lighting system across the set, personal prop | Characters that don't share a system look like fan art |
| **群像 Ensemble** | Ensemble casts, franchises | Pyramid hierarchy with ONE apex; scale = billing order | Democratic spacing kills hierarchy |
| **氛圍／風格版 Atmosphere** | Art-house, festival, boutique release | The world's texture and light; humans small or absent | Pretty but unmemorable without a motif |
| **影展／獎季 Festival & Awards** | Laurels, critic quotes | Restraint; the image steps back so the laurels read | Laurel clutter destroys hierarchy |
| **串流 Key Art** | 16:9 tile, thumbnail-first | Subject off-centre, huge safe margins, title overlay zone left clear | Centre-weighted art gets cropped to mush |

## 3. Concept Extraction (script → one idea)

Run these against the script and keep the strongest single result:

1. **母題法 Motif** — Which object appears at the beginning and returns changed at the end? That object is the poster.
2. **問句法 The Question** — Turn the logline into a question the audience cannot answer from the image alone.
3. **並置法 Juxtaposition** — Put two things in one frame that should never share it (a wedding dress in a morgue, a child's kite over a checkpoint).
4. **缺席法 Absence** — Show the space where the person *was*. An empty chair, a second toothbrush, a dent in a pillow.
5. **尺度法 Scale** — Break the expected size relationship: make the ordinary monumental or the monumental ordinary.

Reject any concept that a synopsis could describe more efficiently than the image.

## 5. Genre → Lighting Recipes

- **Horror** — single hard underlight or a distant practical; 80% of the frame in true black; no fill.
- **Noir / Crime** — hard raking key through blinds or rain-streaked glass; deep shadow; one warm practical as the only relief.
- **Sci-fi / Action** — cool ambient bounce + a hot rim from an off-screen source; atmospheric haze so light beams read.
- **Mystery** — flat overcast ambient with one directional shaft; low contrast until a single accent.
- **Romance** — golden hour backlight, heavy lens bloom, soft falloff, skin lit by bounce.
- **Fantasy / Epic** — volumetric god-rays through particulate; strong rim separating figure from a darker sky.
- **Drama / Biopic** — soft north-window key, believable and unglamorous, shadow side left open.
- **War / Period** — smoke-diffused sun, dust in the air, low sun angle, colour bleached toward the palette.
- **Comedy** — bright even key, minimal shadow, high-key background, saturated accents.
- **Animation / Family** — warm key plus coloured bounce, clean rim, no crushed blacks.

## 6. Motif Library (starting points for §3.1)

Threshold objects: door ajar, keyhole, staircase, window frame · Identity objects: mask, mirror, photograph, uniform, ring · Time objects: clock, wilted flower, dust sheet, cassette · Threat objects: knife shadow, single shoe, empty crib, tyre track · Longing objects: unsent letter, second cup, empty chair, worn ticket · Nature-as-fate: horizon, tide line, cracked earth, single tree.

Choose one and let it appear at a scale that is wrong in an interesting way.

## 7. Tagline Formulas

Chinese ≤12 characters, English ≤10 words. Never explain the plot.

1. **威脅預告** — "有些門，打不開比較好。"
2. **代價句** — "The only way out costs everything."
3. **反轉承諾** — "他來復仇。她在等他。"
4. **時間壓力** — "Seventy-two hours. One witness."
5. **身分問句** — "你認識枕邊的人嗎？"
6. **極簡宣告** — "Some stories bury themselves."

## 8. Common Failures

Floating-head collages · a title fighting the subject's face for the same pixels · subject and background at the same luminance · four or more chromatic families · every element vignetted and glowing to fake depth · a tagline that summarises the plot · symmetry that is nearly symmetrical (commit or abandon) · elements crowding the edges with no breathing margin · an ensemble spaced democratically so nothing leads · a "cinematic" grade applied without a light source that justifies it.

## 9. Quality Gate Details

- **縮圖 Thumbnail** — imagine the frame at 60px wide. If the subject's outer contour dissolves into the background, raise the value separation, not the saturation.
- **灰階 Grayscale** — the subject must sit at least 3 value steps from whatever touches it.
- **三秒 Three-second** — name the idea in one sentence; if the sentence needs an "and", cut one element.
- **類型 Genre** — with the title covered, light + palette alone should place the film within one genre family.
- **無文字 Text-free** — the prompt must reserve a contiguous empty zone of at least 25% of the frame height for the title block.
- **差異 Differentiation** — three archetypes, three scale relationships, three distances; identical palette, light logic and identity anchors.
