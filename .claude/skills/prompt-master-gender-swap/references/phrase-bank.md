# English Clause Bank — Gender Transformation

Ready-to-paste clauses. Pick, adapt to the source, and assemble in the Golden Deconstruction Sequence (SKILL.md §7). Never paste a clause for an occluded feature.

## §1 Rank 1 — Brow ridge & brows

**M→F:** `soften and flatten the prominent supraorbital brow ridge so the area between the eyebrows and the eye sockets becomes smooth and gently rounded, raise the eyebrows so they sit clearly above the orbital rim with a soft natural arch and finer, tidier brow hairs`

**F→M:** `build a pronounced transverse supraorbital brow ridge casting a defined shadow over the eyes, lower the eyebrows to sit directly on the orbital rim, flat and straight with thicker coarser hairs and a fuller tail`

**Androgynous:** `soften the supraorbital ridge to a barely-there transition and set the eyebrows level with the orbital rim, moderately full with only a slight arch`

## §2 Rank 2 — Jaw & chin

**M→F:** `narrow and slightly shorten the chin into a soft rounded taper, soften the square mandibular angle so the jawline curves smoothly and delicately from ear to chin`

**F→M:** `square the mandibular angle toward a right angle with visible masseter flare, widen and lengthen the chin into a broad flat-fronted shape with a subtle mental crease`

## §3 Rank 3 — Neck & shoulder line *(only if visible)*

**M→F:** `narrower shoulder width with a gentle sloping trapezius line, softened acromion points with no bony angularity, delicate shorter collarbones framing a soft supraclavicular hollow, a longer more slender neck with no visible Adam's apple and no prominent sternocleidomastoid cords`

**F→M:** `broader squarer shoulders with a thicker trapezius rising toward the neck root, longer heavier collarbones, a thicker shorter-reading neck with a defined Adam's apple and visible sternocleidomastoid cords`

## §4 Rank 4 — Philtrum & lips

**M→F:** `shorten the philtrum slightly and give the lips a fuller lip red with a defined cupid's bow in a natural rosy tone`

**F→M:** `lengthen the philtrum and thin the lip red into a flatter, less defined vermilion border`

## §5 Rank 5 — Forehead & hairline *(skip if a fringe covers it)*

**M→F:** `reshape the forehead to be more vertical and gently domed with a rounded continuous hairline`

**F→M:** `slope the forehead backward with a flatter transition to the brow and a subtly M-shaped hairline receding at both temples`

## §6 Rank 6–7 — Skin field & midface fat

**M→F:** `completely remove all beard shadow and stubble so the skin reads even, fine-pored and naturally luminous, and lift and round the cheek fat pads for a fuller smoother midface`

**F→M:** `add a faint blue-grey beard shadow across the moustache, chin and jaw field, thicken the skin with coarser visible pores and a slightly more matte finish, and thin the cheeks so the zygomatic arch and masseter read clearly`

## §7 Rank 8–9 — Arms & chest *(only if visible)*

**M→F:** `slim smoothly rounded deltoids with no muscular definition or striation, slender tapering upper arms with a soft continuous contour, a narrower ribcage and a modest natural feminine chest contour`

**F→M:** `squared deltoid caps with visible striation, thicker upper arms with defined biceps and triceps separation, a broader deeper ribcage and a flat wide pectoral plane`

## §8 Hair

- Medium, no fringe: `medium-length hair falling just past the shoulders to the collarbone, worn with no fringe and the forehead fully exposed, softly parted and swept back away from the face, with soft natural volume and individual strands catching the light`
- Blunt fringe: `a blunt straight-cut fringe covering the entire forehead and ending just at eyebrow level, the hairline completely hidden behind the fringe, the side lengths falling smoothly in front of and behind the shoulders`
- Airy fringe fix: `with a slightly airy see-through texture and a few naturally separated strands`
- Short masculine: `a short tapered cut, closely trimmed at the sides and back with slightly longer textured length on top`

## §9 Age

- **Young (≈20):** `smooth taut luminous skin with fine natural pores and soft translucency, even tone continuing from face to neck and shoulders, no nasolabial creases, no forehead or under-eye lines, uniformly natural glossy hair with no grey strands`
- **Mature:** `keep the same fine expression lines, the same delicate silver-grey strands at the temples, and the same softening along the jawline`

## §10 Expression

- **Neutral:** `set the expression to completely neutral and relaxed — lips gently closed and level with no smile, no upturned corners, no cheek lift and no tension anywhere in the face, calm and composed`
- **Stubborn half-smile fix:** `mouth in a completely flat resting line, corners of the mouth angled very slightly downward at rest`
- **Preserve source smile:** `the same faint closed-lip smile with its same subtle asymmetry`

## §11 Identity Lock *(mandatory, adapt every clause to the source)*

`Everything else remains exactly as in the original: the same [ethnicity/age] person with the identical eye shape — [narrow / round / almond], [hooded / creased] eyes with the same lid crease and the same inter-ocular distance, the same [colour] irises and the same steady direct gaze into the lens — the same [straight / curved] nose with unchanged bridge width, tip shape and nostril form, the same [warm ivory] skin tone, the same [named marks: mole / freckles / scar] in the same positions, the same head squared frontally to camera and the same relaxed level shoulder line`

**Reinforcement clause** (prepend to the whole prompt when likeness drifts):
`Preserve the subject's facial identity and likeness above all else; this is the same individual, only [younger and female / older and male].`

## §12 Wardrobe, background & cascade

- Keep garment: `wearing the identical [garment] with the same [neckline] and the same soft fabric folds`
- New garment: `change the clothing to [garment], [fabric], [neckline sitting well above the chest], leaving the [shoulders, collarbones and upper arms] bare`
- Backdrop fill after narrowing: `the backdrop extended cleanly to fill the space revealed around the narrower frame`
- Tonal separation: `with clear tonal separation keeping the [black] garment readable against the [grey] background`

## §13 Medium, lighting & realism

`Rendered as a clean professional studio portrait with the same large soft frontal key light, soft wrap-around fill, gentle shadow falloff beneath the jaw and neck, neutral white balance, and natural photographic skin texture with visible fine pores, real fine vellus hair and no plastic smoothing, waxy sheen or heavy retouching, matching the exact original photographic medium, colour grading, tonal contrast and depth of field`

**Illustrated source instead:** `rendered in the exact original art medium — the same [line weight / cel shading / brushwork], the same palette and the same rendering density; do not convert toward photorealism`

## §14 Framing

- **Lock:** `keep the exact same [chest-up bust] framing, crop, camera height and subject scale — do not zoom in, zoom out, widen, re-center, or extend the composition`
- **Declared reframe:** `Reframe closer than the original: push in to a tight chest-up close-up so the head fills significantly more of the frame, cropping the bottom edge across the upper chest just below the armpits, keeping the same eye-level camera height, the same squared frontal angle and the head centered with a small margin of headroom`
