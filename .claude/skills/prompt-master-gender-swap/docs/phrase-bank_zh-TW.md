# 英文子句庫 —— 性別轉換（中文導讀版）

> 說明：本檔為 `references/phrase-bank.md` 的繁體中文導讀。**英文子句本身即是交付物，故一律保留原文不譯**；每則下方附中文說明其作用與使用時機。

可直接貼用的子句。挑選、依原圖調整，再依「黃金解構順序」（SKILL.md §7）組裝。絕不為被遮蔽的特徵貼上子句。

## §1 第 1 級 —— 眉骨與眉毛
> 全場權重最高的一句。壓平眶上脊 + 移動眉毛相對於眼窩緣的位置，是單一最有效的性別訊號。

**男→女：** `soften and flatten the prominent supraorbital brow ridge so the area between the eyebrows and the eye sockets becomes smooth and gently rounded, raise the eyebrows so they sit clearly above the orbital rim with a soft natural arch and finer, tidier brow hairs`

**女→男：** `build a pronounced transverse supraorbital brow ridge casting a defined shadow over the eyes, lower the eyebrows to sit directly on the orbital rim, flat and straight with thicker coarser hairs and a fuller tail`

**中性：** `soften the supraorbital ridge to a barely-there transition and set the eyebrows level with the orbital rim, moderately full with only a slight arch`

## §2 第 2 級 —— 下顎與下巴
> 決定臉的外輪廓。與第 1 級並列為「就算只寫兩句也要寫這兩句」。

**男→女：** `narrow and slightly shorten the chin into a soft rounded taper, soften the square mandibular angle so the jawline curves smoothly and delicately from ear to chin`

**女→男：** `square the mandibular angle toward a right angle with visible masseter flare, widen and lengthen the chin into a broad flat-fronted shape with a subtle mental crease`

## §3 第 3 級 —— 頸部與肩線（僅在可見時）
> 胸上構圖的勝負手。只改臉不改肩，成品會有強烈違和感。

**男→女：** `narrower shoulder width with a gentle sloping trapezius line, softened acromion points with no bony angularity, delicate shorter collarbones framing a soft supraclavicular hollow, a longer more slender neck with no visible Adam's apple and no prominent sternocleidomastoid cords`

**女→男：** `broader squarer shoulders with a thicker trapezius rising toward the neck root, longer heavier collarbones, a thicker shorter-reading neck with a defined Adam's apple and visible sternocleidomastoid cords`

## §4 第 4 級 —— 人中與唇
> 人中長度是被低估的性別線索；唇厚反而是次要的。

**男→女：** `shorten the philtrum slightly and give the lips a fuller lip red with a defined cupid's bow in a natural rosy tone`

**女→男：** `lengthen the philtrum and thin the lip red into a flatter, less defined vermilion border`

## §5 第 5 級 —— 額頭與髮際線（有瀏海遮住時跳過）
> ⚠️ 若指定齊劉海，這一整段作廢，權重轉移至 §1／§2／§3。

**男→女：** `reshape the forehead to be more vertical and gently domed with a rounded continuous hairline`

**女→男：** `slope the forehead backward with a flatter transition to the brow and a subtly M-shaped hairline receding at both temples`

## §6 第 6–7 級 —— 膚質區與中臉脂肪
> 只做這一級（例如單純去鬍青）會讀成「刮了鬍子的男人」，務必搭配 §1／§2。

**男→女：** `completely remove all beard shadow and stubble so the skin reads even, fine-pored and naturally luminous, and lift and round the cheek fat pads for a fuller smoother midface`

**女→男：** `add a faint blue-grey beard shadow across the moustache, chin and jaw field, thicken the skin with coarser visible pores and a slightly more matte finish, and thin the cheeks so the zygomatic arch and masseter read clearly`

## §7 第 8–9 級 —— 手臂與胸部（僅在可見時）
> 露肩服裝會讓這一級從「不可見」變成「必寫」。

**男→女：** `slim smoothly rounded deltoids with no muscular definition or striation, slender tapering upper arms with a soft continuous contour, a narrower ribcage and a modest natural feminine chest contour`

**女→男：** `squared deltoid caps with visible striation, thicker upper arms with defined biceps and triceps separation, a broader deeper ribcage and a flat wide pectoral plane`

## §8 髮型

- 中長、無瀏海：`medium-length hair falling just past the shoulders to the collarbone, worn with no fringe and the forehead fully exposed, softly parted and swept back away from the face, with soft natural volume and individual strands catching the light`
- 齊劉海：`a blunt straight-cut fringe covering the entire forehead and ending just at eyebrow level, the hairline completely hidden behind the fringe, the side lengths falling smoothly in front of and behind the shoulders`
- 假髮感修正（空氣感）：`with a slightly airy see-through texture and a few naturally separated strands`
- 男性短髮：`a short tapered cut, closely trimmed at the sides and back with slightly longer textured length on top`

## §9 年齡

- **年輕（約 20 歲）：** `smooth taut luminous skin with fine natural pores and soft translucency, even tone continuing from face to neck and shoulders, no nasolabial creases, no forehead or under-eye lines, uniformly natural glossy hair with no grey strands`
- **成熟：** `keep the same fine expression lines, the same delicate silver-grey strands at the temples, and the same softening along the jawline`

## §10 表情

- **中性：** `set the expression to completely neutral and relaxed — lips gently closed and level with no smile, no upturned corners, no cheek lift and no tension anywhere in the face, calm and composed`
- **殘笑修正：** `mouth in a completely flat resting line, corners of the mouth angled very slightly downward at rest`
- **保留原微笑：** `the same faint closed-lip smile with its same subtle asymmetry`

## §11 身分鎖定（必附，每個子句都要依原圖調整）

`Everything else remains exactly as in the original: the same [ethnicity/age] person with the identical eye shape — [narrow / round / almond], [hooded / creased] eyes with the same lid crease and the same inter-ocular distance, the same [colour] irises and the same steady direct gaze into the lens — the same [straight / curved] nose with unchanged bridge width, tip shape and nostril form, the same [warm ivory] skin tone, the same [named marks: mole / freckles / scar] in the same positions, the same head squared frontally to camera and the same relaxed level shoulder line`

**強化句**（當神似度開始漂移時，加在整段提示詞最前面）：
`Preserve the subject's facial identity and likeness above all else; this is the same individual, only [younger and female / older and male].`

## §12 服裝、背景與連鎖

- 保留服裝：`wearing the identical [garment] with the same [neckline] and the same soft fabric folds`
- 更換服裝：`change the clothing to [garment], [fabric], [neckline sitting well above the chest], leaving the [shoulders, collarbones and upper arms] bare`
- 收窄後背景填補：`the backdrop extended cleanly to fill the space revealed around the narrower frame`
- 明度分離：`with clear tonal separation keeping the [black] garment readable against the [grey] background`

## §13 媒材、光線與真實感

`Rendered as a clean professional studio portrait with the same large soft frontal key light, soft wrap-around fill, gentle shadow falloff beneath the jaw and neck, neutral white balance, and natural photographic skin texture with visible fine pores, real fine vellus hair and no plastic smoothing, waxy sheen or heavy retouching, matching the exact original photographic medium, colour grading, tonal contrast and depth of field`

**插畫來源改用：** `rendered in the exact original art medium — the same [line weight / cel shading / brushwork], the same palette and the same rendering density; do not convert toward photorealism`

## §14 構圖

- **鎖定：** `keep the exact same [chest-up bust] framing, crop, camera height and subject scale — do not zoom in, zoom out, widen, re-center, or extend the composition`
- **宣告式改景別：** `Reframe closer than the original: push in to a tight chest-up close-up so the head fills significantly more of the frame, cropping the bottom edge across the upper chest just below the armpits, keeping the same eye-level camera height, the same squared frontal angle and the head centered with a small margin of headroom`

## §15 微調開關樣板

- 模型仍留殘笑 → 把 `no smile, no upturned corners` 換成 §10 的殘笑修正句。
- 臉被改成通用美人 → 句首插入 §11 的強化句。
- 想要中性／半轉 → 刪除 §4 與 §7，並把 §1、§2 的動詞換成 `slightly`。
- 肩膀收太過 → 刪除 `a narrower ribcage,`，並把 `narrower shoulder width` 改為 `slightly narrower shoulder width`。
- 想維持原景別 → 把 §14 的 reframe 句換回 lock 句。
- 髮型假髮感 → 在髮型句後加 §8 的空氣感修正句。
