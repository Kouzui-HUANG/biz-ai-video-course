## 1. Archetype cards

Each card: **Skeleton** · **Canon** · **Why it works** · **Best for** · **Risk → Fix**.

### A1 Condition-twist 條件反轉型
- **Skeleton:** 〜したら、〜だった（件）／〜に生まれ変わった俺は〜／轉生成〜／一覺醒來，我成了〜／~했더니 ~였다
- **Canon:** 転生したらスライムだった件 · 転生したら剣でした · 自動販売機に生まれ変わった俺は迷宮を彷徨う
- **Why:** premise ＋ surprising noun = an instant image plus the question "how does that even work?"
- **Best for:** isekai, reincarnation, body-swap, 穿越／穿書
- **Risk → Fix:** the most saturated template; a predictable noun drowns → the noun must be lower or odder than anything in the genre's current top titles, and must carry a handicap (T3).

### A2 Cause → decision 因果宣言型
- **Skeleton:** 〜されたので、〜することにしました／被〜的我，決定〜／〜後，我〜了／~당해서 ~하기로 했다
- **Canon:** 真の仲間じゃないと勇者のパーティーを追い出されたので、辺境でスローライフすることにしました · 追放されたチート付与魔術師は気ままなセカンドライフを謳歌する。
- **Why:** an injustice creates grievance; the decision promises the payoff.
- **Best for:** exile, broken engagement, revenge, slow life, comeback, CN micro-drama
- **Risk → Fix:** タイトル詐欺 (F4) and length (F6) → the decision verb must be the core loop; cut to the market cap.

### A3 Denial / rhetorical 反語否認型
- **Skeleton:** 〜がこんなに〜わけがない／やはり〜はまちがっている。／〜のは間違っているだろうか／〜哪有這麼〜／〜才不是〜／~할 리가 없어
- **Canon:** 俺の妹がこんなに可愛いわけがない · やはり俺の青春ラブコメはまちがっている。 · ダンジョンに出会いを求めるのは間違っているだろうか
- **Why:** the title performs the narrator's denial while the reader knows the opposite is true — irony, tsundere energy, genre-savvy comedy.
- **Best for:** romcom, school, self-aware comedy
- **Risk → Fix:** a vague target (「人生哪有這麼難」) → deny something concrete and slightly embarrassing.

### A4 Character close-up 角色特寫型
- **Skeleton:** 〔frequency／habit〕＋〔secret quirk〕＋〔relational position 隣の／クラスの／上司の〕＋〔name ＋ honorific〕
- **Canon:** 時々ボソッとロシア語でデレる隣のアーリャさん · 弱キャラ友崎くん · 政宗くんのリベンジ
- **Why:** names a person the reader will fall for; the secret quirk creates dramatic irony (the reader is in on it).
- **Best for:** romcom, slice-of-life, character-driven series
- **Risk → Fix:** a generic quirk (「很可愛的」) → the quirk must be specific and secret.

### A5 Retort / talk-back 嗆聲對話型
- **Skeleton:** 〜ですが、なにか?／〜したいと思います。／〜又怎樣？／吃了我的〜，還想〜？／~인데요, 뭐?
- **Canon:** 蜘蛛ですが、なにか? · 痛いのは嫌なので防御力に極振りしたいと思います。
- **Why:** the protagonist's attitude speaks before page one; the attitude becomes the brand.
- **Best for:** comedy, overpowered-but-quirky heroes, CN／KR short-drama hooks
- **Risk → Fix:** attitude clashes with a dark or serious work → use only if the narration really sounds like this.

### A6 Stat / rule 數值・規則型
- **Skeleton:** Lv.／9999／SSS級／極振り／ステータス／ガチャ／〜的規則：〜／나 혼자만 ~
- **Canon:** 信じていた仲間達に…ギフト『無限ガチャ』でレベル9999の仲間達を…（無限ガチャ） · 나 혼자만 레벨업 · 防振り
- **Why:** game literacy makes numbers and rules instantly legible; a stated rule invites "what if it is broken?"
- **Best for:** game systems, hunters, dungeons, rule-horror（規則怪談）, gimmick dramas
- **Risk → Fix:** number inflation (99999…) and a templated feel → one number or rule, tied to the anchor noun.

### A7 Idiom subversion 熟語改造型
- **Skeleton:** a phrase the audience knows by heart, with one element swapped
- **Canon:** 전지적 독자 시점 (← 전지적 작가 시점, the literary term "omniscient author's viewpoint") · (illustrative) 炸後吐真言 (← 酒後吐真言)
- **Why:** familiarity ＋ dislocation = the most memorable short form; usually short enough to be its own handle.
- **Best for:** any genre; strongest in the short／brand slot
- **Risk → Fix:** the source phrase is unknown in the market or untranslatable → pick a phrase every reader of that language knows; plan a separate subversion for each export market.

### A8 Elegant noun phrase 雅名型
- **Skeleton:** 〔job／place／object〕の〔noun〕／a 2–4 character image／〔name〕＋〔relation〕
- **Canon:** 薬屋のひとりごと · 本好きの下剋上 · 魔法科高校の劣等生 · 長風渡 · 墨雨雲間 · 폭군의 셰프 · 재벌집 막내아들
- **Why:** brandable, poster-ready, prestige-safe; carries the work when it wins on character and word of mouth (薬屋のひとりごと passed 45 million copies with a seven-character title).
- **Best for:** TV／film／anime crossover, awards, literary-leaning, mystery or court works; the short half of a dual name
- **Risk → Fix:** lower click-through on list UIs and drift into abstraction (F7) → keep the anchor noun inside it (薬屋, 劣等生, 셰프).

### A9 Main ＋ subtitle 主副標型
- **Skeleton:** 〔brand title〕 ～〔one-clause synopsis〕～
- **Canon:** 無職転生 ～異世界行ったら本気だす～ · 本好きの下剋上 ～司書になるためには手段を選んでいられません～
- **Why:** brand and blurb in one; the subtitle can be dropped for anime, TV or merchandise.
- **Best for:** web serials planning to cross media; long titles that need a handle
- **Risk → Fix:** the subtitle bloats into F6 → subtitle ≤ one clause, and the main title must pass the gate on its own.

### A10 Platform-native (CN／KR) 平台在地型
- **Skeleton (簡中):** 〔類型前綴〕：〔一句話簡介〕／開局〜／我在〜當・學〜／重生後，〜／穿成〜的〜／〜後，〜／〜駕到／〜馬甲掉了
- **Skeleton (繁中):** 〜的我，〜／明明是〜，卻〜／〜時〜，長大後〜／關於我〜這件事／〔setup〕，〔payoff〕
- **Skeleton (KR):** ~로 살아남기／~에 빙의했다／~가 되었다／회귀한 ~／나 혼자만 ~
- **Canon:** 我在精神病院學斬神 · 十八歲太奶奶駕到，重整家族榮耀 · 三歲時被父母遺棄在副本，長大後成為副本主人並殺死進來的獵人們 · 연산군의 셰프로 살아남기
- **Why:** matches the platform's native reading rhythm and search habits; the micro-drama two-clause form is setup ＋ payoff.
- **Best for:** 番茄／起點／飛盧／晉江 serials, 微短劇, KakaoPage／Naver Series
- **Risk → Fix:** regulatory red lines (F10) and saturation (F5) → check the market red-line list; the anchor noun must be unusual.

## 2. Genre → archetype fit matrix

| Genre | First choice | Also strong | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Isekai／reincarnation 異世界・轉生 | A1 | A2, A9, A6 | — |
| Exile／revenge 追放・復仇・打臉 | A2 | A6, A10 | A3 |
| Romcom／school 戀愛喜劇・校園 | A3, A4 | A5, A7 | A6 |
| Villainess／female-oriented fantasy 惡役千金・女性向奇幻 | A1, A2 | A8, A10 | — |
| Court／historical 古裝・宮鬥 | A8 | A2, A10 | A6 |
| Mystery／suspense 懸疑・推理 | A8, A7 | A6 (as a rule), A5 | A1／A2 if they expose the mechanism |
| Healing／urban fantasy／supernatural slice-of-life 療癒・都會奇幻・靈異日常 | A4, A8 | A7, A3, A5, A6 (as a price or rule) | A2 when the material has no grievance |
| Craft／food／shop slice-of-life 職人・美食・店舖日常 | A4, A8 | A2, A7 | — |
| Game／hunter／dungeon 遊戲・獵人 | A6 | A1, A2 | — |
| Urban comeback／identity reversal 都市逆襲・身分反轉 | A10, A2 | A5, A7 | A8 as the platform title (use it only as the cross-media name) |
| Horror／rules 恐怖・規則怪談 | A6 | A8, A5 | A3 |

- The matrix ranks; it does not forbid. "Avoid" means: use only with the reason stated in 風險與對策.
- Micro-drama, anime and TV are media, not genres: pick archetypes by genre, then apply the medium overlay (market-playbook §2). For mixed genres, blend the matching rows.

## 3. Set-composition rules

1. One archetype per proposal; no duplicates.
2. Spread the lengths with the applicable bands (market-playbook §1–2): ≥1 long-band title (discovery), ≥1 short-band title (brand), and no band holding more than half the set.
3. Whenever cross-media is plausible (anime／TV／film／short-drama IP), include one A8 or A7 — it doubles as the dual name.
4. At most one proposal may use a cliché template (〜件／這件事).
5. Each title uses its own best anchor; across the set use ≥2 different anchors, so the user chooses between angles, not wordings (title-anatomy T1).
6. Each proposal answers a different "why click": the gap (A1, A7), the grievance (A2), the person (A3, A4), the attitude (A5), the rule or price (A6), the prestige (A8), the whole picture at once (A9), the platform's native hook (A10 — classify it by what its clauses carry). Archetypes that share a why-click may appear together only with different anchors.
7. A3 vs A5 tie-break: denying a fact about oneself or the work = A3; talking back to someone (a customer, the reader, the world) = A5.
