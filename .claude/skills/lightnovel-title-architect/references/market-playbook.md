## 1. Defaults and length bands
- **Market** = the user's language market unless one is named (繁體中文 → Taiwan).
- **Medium** inferred from the material: script with scenes → 短劇／影視; chaptered serial → web novel; anime／manga／game pitch → JP-style LN.
- **Audience** inferred from the genre (romance fantasy → female-oriented; game／hunter → male-oriented).
- **【企劃假設】** records every item inferred rather than stated in the material: market, platform, medium, audience, and anything treated as premise (title-anatomy T4).

**Length bands.** Count every character, punctuation included; Japanese counts 字, Korean counts syllables (spaces excluded). A medium overlay (§2) replaces these bands when it gives its own.

| Market | Short (brand／cross-media) | Mid | Long (platform) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Japan | ≤ 10字 | 11–25字 | 26–40字 |
| 繁中 (Taiwan／HK) | ≤ 6字 | 7–15字 | 16–25字 |
| 簡中 web serial | ≤ 6字 | 7–14字 | 15–20字 |
| Korea | ≤ 8 syllables | 9–14 | 15–20 |

A title above the long band fails G7 unless it is kept deliberately (failure-gate §3).

## 2. Medium overlays (any market)
- **直式短劇／微短劇 (any Chinese market):** bands become short ≤ 6字 · mid 7–10字 · long 11–16字. A title over 10字 splits into two beats with a comma or colon (setup，payoff), and the hook lands in the first beat, which survives a thumbnail crop. A longer platform caption may be mentioned under 最適媒介 but is never the series title. An episode-1 title may quote the opening line of dialogue (A5) as an extra beside the series title.
- **Web serial:** use the market bands; A9 subtitles are welcome; plan the handle from day one.
- **Anime／manga／game:** keep the platform title and announce an official 略稱 with it (「略して #無限ガチャ」).
- **TV／film／awards／festivals:** an elegant or short name (A8／A7) is mandatory; the platform title becomes secondary.

## 3. Japan
- **Platforms:** 小説家になろう, カクヨム → 単行本 or 文庫 → anime. Web-origin books are now the bigger paper segment (2024 paper LN ¥18.3 bn = 単行本 10.0 ＋ 文庫 8.3).
- **Length:** なろう rankings cluster at 31–40字 (two phone lines) and readability drops beyond 40. Book／anime main titles usually sit in the mid band with a 4-mora handle; brand names in the short band (薬屋のひとりごと 8, 本好きの下剋上 7). Across 3,669 LN titles: mean 16字, median 13; web-origin 17.3 vs others 14.9; length barely correlates with success (r ≈ 0.07).
- **Native archetypes:** A1–A6, A9. Endings: 件／わけがない／だろうか／ことにしました／ですが、なにか?／と思います。／します！
- **Notes:** critics' rankings favour short titles (このライトノベルがすごい！2026: あそびのかんけい, サイレント・ウィッチ). Budget 〜な件 (title-anatomy §4).

## 4. Traditional Chinese (Taiwan／Hong Kong)
- **Channels:** translated LN (東立, 台灣角川, 尖端, 青文) and local platforms (KadoKado 角角者). The 2025 KADOKAWA × 台灣角川 contest guarantees JP ＋ 繁中 editions, so 繁中 writers increasingly title for both markets.
- **Native templates** (translation-derived, familiar to readers): 關於我〜這件事／〜哪有這麼〜／〜是否搞錯了什麼／果然〜搞錯了。／〜的我，〜／明明是〜，卻〜／〜才不是〜／〜時〜，長大後〜／〔setup〕，〔payoff〕.
- **Avoid** mainland-only platform slang (開局、打錢、全民) unless targeting the mainland, and mainland vocabulary (視頻→影片, 質量→品質).
- **Local anchors** read strongly: 夜市, 廟口, 便當, 補習班, 捷運, 超商大夜班.

## 5. Simplified Chinese (mainland)
- **Data:** 晉江 hit titles averaged 4.28字 in 2015 and 7.08字 in 2024; after 2020 top works left the four-character era. 飛盧 = 類型前綴 ＋ 冒號 ＋ one-line synopsis. 番茄 A/B-tests titles (測書), so titles are ad copy. 起點's top tier is still mostly four-character titles by veteran authors.
- **Native templates:** 〔前綴〕：〔簡介〕／開局〜／我在〜當・學〜／重生之〜／重生後，〜／穿成〜的〜／全民〜／〜後，〜（離婚後／閃婚後）／〜駕到／〜馬甲掉了. Micro-drama titles run on an identity gap (十八歲太奶奶駕到，重整家族榮耀); form per §2.
- **Red lines (國家廣電總局 notices):** 2024-11-22 — do not use 霸總-type words as the title hook; 2024-12-20 — strengthened micro-drama title review against sensational or vulgar titles; 2025-03-12 — 「爽」而有度. Avoid: 霸總, money／flaunting／power-worship hooks (拜金・炫富・炫權), sexual innuendo, glorified violence or revenge, superstition, discriminatory labels.
- **Prestige track:** TV adaptations restore an elegant title — 嫁紈絝 → 長風渡, 嫡嫁千金 → 墨雨雲間, 本王在此 → 與鳳行. Always supply one for IP-bound projects.

## 6. Korea
- **Data:** 48,530 Naver Series titles (2011–2022) show sentence titles steadily lengthening, fastest in 현대판타지 and 로맨스판타지; classic romance moves slower (KCI, 2023).
- **Lexicon (Sino-Korean):** 회귀・빙의・환생・악녀・악역・공작・황태자・재벌・막내아들・헌터・SSS급・천재・귀환.
- **Native templates:** ~로 살아남기／~에 빙의했다／~가 되었다／회귀한 ~／나 혼자만 ~／~리가 없어／~하기로 했다／~건에 대하여.
- **Sweet spot:** a short sentence of 6–12 syllables (나 혼자만 레벨업, 재벌집 막내아들, 김비서가 왜 그럴까).
- **TV rename:** 연산군의 셰프로 살아남기 → 폭군의 셰프 (tvN 2025; the real king became a fictional one).

## 7. Vietnam and English (only when named)
- **Vietnam:** publishers often shorten long titles (Kim Đồng: お隣の天使様にいつの間にか駄目人間にされていた件 → Thiên Sứ Nhà Bên). Hán-Việt genre words travel well: xuyên không (穿越), trọng sinh (重生), hệ thống (系統), ngôn tình (言情). Prefer short, anchor-led titles.
- **English:** long translated sentence titles are accepted (That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime); provide an acronym-friendly form.

## 8. Handle (略稱) rules

| Language | Rule | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Japanese | First two mora of two key words → four mora; or the anchor noun itself | 転スラ・防振り・ロシデレ・ダンまち・俺妹／薬屋・無限ガチャ |
| Korean | First syllable of each word | 나혼렙・전독시 |
| Chinese | One character from each of two key words, or the most distinctive noun／verb | 地錯（地下城＋搞錯）・春物（青春＋物語）・斬神・凡人 |

- **略稱** (the spoken nickname): ≤ 4 mora／characters／syllables; sayable in one breath; returns this work on search. If it is a bare common word, always pair it with a title-bound hashtag.
- **Hashtag:** the 略稱, or the whole title when the title is in the short band.
- **A short-band title may serve as its own 略稱.**
- Announce the handle together with the title.

## 9. Dual-name strategy
- **Platform title** (long band; A1／A2／A6／A10) for discovery ＋ **brand／elegant name** (short band; A7／A8) for posters, TV, merchandise and awards.
- **Required** for every long-band title; for mid-band titles only when cross-media is planned.
- The short name must reuse the long title's anchor or core image so audiences connect the two (폭군의 셰프 kept 셰프).
- Always state which name serves which medium.
