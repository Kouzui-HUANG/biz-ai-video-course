## 1. The six slots (+ handle)

A light-novel-style title is assembled from up to six slots. Winning titles fill **2–4**. Filling only ① = keyword salad (F1); filling all six = synopsis dump (F6). Count only explicit words — an implied genre does not fill ①. One word may fill two slots; label it with both numbers (②③無職, ②③超商大夜店員).

| Slot | Job | Typical fillers |
|---|---|---|
| ① Genre key 類型鑰匙 | Lets the reader identify the genre in about one second | 転生・追放・悪役令嬢・婚約破棄 / 重生・穿成・開局 / 회귀・빙의・환생 |
| ② Plight 身分／處境 | Who the hero is and where they are stuck | 無職・劣等生・被退婚的聖女・接手攤位的大學生 |
| ③ Anchor noun 錨點名詞 | One concrete, drawable noun that belongs only to this work — its "face" | スライム・無限ガチャ・薬屋・精神病院・太奶奶 |
| ④ Mechanism 機制／規則 | The power, rule or constraint | 防御力に極振り・ロシア語でデレる・Re:（hinting a restart） |
| ⑤ Promise 承諾 | The pleasure the reader will get, again and again | ざまぁ・スローライフ・本気だす・下剋上・溺愛・打臉 |
| ⑥ Voice 聲口 | Ending, person, punctuation — a free sample of the narration | 〜件・〜わけがない・〜ですが、なにか?・。・！ |
| ⑦ Handle 略稱 (outside the title) | The 2–4 unit nickname / hashtag | 転スラ・俺妹・나혼렙・斬神 |

## 3. Semantic techniques T1–T6

**T1 Anchor.** A concrete noun or noun phrase — places and times count (大夜班, 凌晨三點) — scored on three tests: **U**nique to this work (swap test), **D**rawable as a thumbnail, carries **T**ension (unexpected in this genre). Score ≥3 candidates and give each title its strongest one; the set as a whole needs ≥2 different anchors, and a secondary anchor must still pass U and D. In an idiom subversion (T6) the anchor may live in the swapped element (炸 → 雞排). スライム beats 魔物; 精神病院 beats 奇怪的地方; 雞排 beats 食物.

**T2 Gap pairing.** Join the two most semantically distant elements of the material.

| Gap axis | Examples |
|---|---|
| Low status × high stakes | 無職×転生 · 劣等生×魔法科 · 司書×下剋上 |
| Mundane × epic | 精神病院×斬神 · 薬屋×後宮の謎 |
| Age / role inversion | 十八歲×太奶奶 · 魔王×アルバイト（はたらく魔王さま!） |
| Genre fusion | ダンジョン×出会い（ダンまち） |
| Secret × public | ロシア語でデレる — only the hero understands |

**T3 Problem first.** The title must hold a handicap, cost, curse, constraint or irony. A pure tool or power states the answer before the question exists. Formulas: *power ＋ constraint* (防御力に極振り) or *identity ＋ defect* (劣等生, 悪役令嬢). Counter-case: 異世界はスマートフォンとともに。(F2).

**T4 Hide the payload.** State the premise and the pleasure type; never the twist, the ending or the hidden core mechanism. Hint instead with a symbol (Re:), a euphemism (育成計画 masking a death game in 魔法少女育成計画), a question (〜だろうか), or a rule stated without its consequence. **Premise vs payload:** anything revealed in episode 1 or chapter 1 (roughly the first tenth of the story) is premise and may enter the title; only its later consequence — the twist it causes — goes on the do-not-reveal list. A memory-for-wishes price shown in episode 1 is premise; the forgotten person it leads to is payload.

**T5 Promise = core loop.** A promise word must name what happens episode after episode, proven by a quote from the material.

| Pleasure | JP | 中文 | KR |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenge / face-slap | ざまぁ・見返す | 打臉・逆襲・復仇 | 복수・참교육・사이다 |
| Slow life | スローライフ・のんびり | 慢活・種田 | 힐링 |
| Overpower | 無双・最強・チート | 無敵・外掛・開掛 | 먼치킨 |
| Rise | 成り上がり・下剋上 | 翻身・逆襲 | 성장 |
| Doom avoidance | 破滅フラグ回避 | 改命・避開 BE | 생존 |
| Doting romance | 溺愛 | 寵・偏愛 | 집착・후회 |
| Healing／heartwarming | ほっこり・癒やし | 療癒・暖心 | 힐링・감동 |
| Bittersweet／tearjerker | 泣ける・切ない | 催淚・虐心 | 눈물・신파 |

**Episodic visitor loop** (shops, inns, clinics, night shifts): the promise is the ritual itself — name the venue together with its hour or rule (深夜食堂, 薬屋のひとりごと, 大夜班).

**T6 Idiom subversion.** Take a phrase the audience knows by heart and swap one element: 전지적 작가 시점 → 전지적 독자 시점; (illustrative) 酒後吐真言 → 炸後吐真言. Works only if the source phrase is common in the target language — never translate a subverted idiom literally; build a new one per market.

## 4. Voice — endings, punctuation, person

**Origin.** The form imitates a forum thread title (2ch VIP スレタイ): a first-person, deadpan report you want to click. 〜な件 reached commercial LN titles around 2011.

| Move | JP | 繁中 | 簡中 | KR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Report | 〜だった件 | 關於我〜這件事 | 关于我〜这件事 | ~건에 대하여 |
| Denial | 〜わけがない | 〜哪有這麼〜 | 〜哪有这么〜 | ~리가 없어 |
| Question | 〜だろうか | 〜是否搞錯了什麼 | 〜是否搞错了什么 | ~걸까 |
| Decision | 〜ことにしました | 於是我決定〜 | 于是我决定〜 | ~하기로 했다 |
| Retort | 〜ですが、なにか? | 〜又怎樣？ | 〜又怎样？ | ~인데요, 뭐? |

**Punctuation is attitude.**

| Mark | Effect | Canon |
|---|---|---|
| 。 | Deadpan verdict, resignation | やはり俺の青春ラブコメはまちがっている。 |
| ！ | Comic energy, exclamation | この素晴らしい世界に祝福を! · 負けヒロインが多すぎる! |
| ？ | Invites the reader to answer | ダンまち · 蜘蛛ですが、なにか? |
| … | Helplessness, trailing sigh | 〜悪役令嬢に転生してしまった… |
| 〜と思います。 | Polite register for absurd content | 防振り |
| ～subtitle～ | Brand ／ blurb split | 無職転生 ～異世界行ったら本気だす～ |

**Person & honorifics.** 俺 (male-oriented, brash) · 僕 (soft) · 私 (female-oriented or formal) · 本王／本宮 (rank as voice). 〜さん・〜くん・〜様 set the distance between narrator and target.

**Cliché budget.** 〜件 is rarer than its reputation — only 20 published LN titles used 〜な件 by early 2019 — so it now reads as a template marker. Use 〜件／這件事／這檔事／건에 대하여 at most once per set, and only when the narration is genuinely a deadpan report.
