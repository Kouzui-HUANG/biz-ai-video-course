## 1. Failure catalog

| # | Failure | Real case | Why it fails | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Keyword salad 標籤沙拉 | 異世界チート魔術師 — three genre tags, no anchor. The 2019 anime averaged about 2.8／5 across ~1,000 Filmarks reviews; a common complaint was a "cheat" hero with no visible cheat | Fits any work in the genre = no face; unmemorable, unsearchable | Swap test; add one anchor noun unique to the work |
| F2 | Answer without a problem 只給答案 | 異世界はスマートフォンとともに。 — the anchor is an all-powerful tool, so the title promises no obstacle; the hero was nicknamed 「スマホ太郎」 and the show became shorthand for なろう-style criticism (yet it got a 2023 season 2: notoriety caps crossover rather than killing sales) | No "how will they get out of this?" | Give the anchor a cost, limit or irony: power ＋ constraint (防振り), identity ＋ defect (劣等生) |
| F3 | Spoiler 洩底 | Titles that state the ending — listed by 公募ガイド among the five things never to put in an LN title | Reader feels there is no need to read | Say premise ＋ pleasure type; hide mechanism and ending. Revenge genres' ざまぁ promises an emotion, not an outcome — not a spoiler |
| F4 | Broken promise タイトル詐欺 | The long-running reader complaint 「スローライフ詐欺」: titles promise slow life, content is non-stop battles. Some authors append 「（願望）」 in self-mockery. F1's case also failed its 「チート」 | The more specific the title, the harsher the backlash when it is not honoured | Verify each promise word against the material's core loop (evidence quote) |
| F5 | Copycat collision 跟風撞名 | The 「俺の〜」 wave after 俺妹 (2011–13: 俺修羅, のうコメ); the 2019–21 「追放」 wave — in 2021 the three longest なろう titles were all exile／firing stories | Once a template trends it stops differentiating; only the filler competes | Borrow the skeleton but make the filler stranger than the original (転生したら剣でした, 自動販売機に生まれ変わった俺は迷宮を彷徨う); vary within the set |
| F6 | Synopsis dump 簡介倒灌 | なろう's longest title reached 134 characters (2021) | Exceeds two phone lines, no break point; won't fit a spine, poster or hashtag | Keep 2–4 slots; split into main ＋ subtitle; publish an official handle (無限ガチャ model). Length alone is not fatal: 追放されたチート付与魔術師… stalled at 2 novel volumes yet its manga reached 21 volumes and an October 2026 anime — content and medium decided it |
| F7 | Abstraction 抽象空泛 | Titles like 「希望之光」「新的旅程」 | No image; anyone could use it | Adjective → noun → proper noun |
| F8 | Jargon soup 術語亂燉 | Several coined terms from different genres in one title | Reader cannot even tell the genre | One coined term per title; carry the rest with known words |
| F9 | Platform mismatch 平台錯配 | Long web titles renamed for TV: 嫁紈絝 → 長風渡, 嫡嫁千金 → 墨雨雲間, 연산군의 셰프로 살아남기 → 폭군의 셰프 | A list-UI title does not suit posters, TV or awards | Plan an elegant／short name from day one |
| F10 | Regulatory red line 監管紅線 | NRTA 2024-11-22: no 霸總-type words as title hooks; 2024-12-20: stricter micro-drama title review | A click-optimised title collides with censorship | Apply the market red-line list (market-playbook §5) |
| F11 | Export fragmentation 出口斷裂 | お隣の天使様にいつの間にか駄目人間にされていた件 → 東立《關於我在無意間被隔壁的天使變成廢柴這件事》, another 繁中 rendering 《關於鄰家的天使大人不知不覺把我慣成了廢人這檔子事》, Vietnamese "Thiên Sứ Nhà Bên" | One work, several names: the brand splits | Keep the anchor translatable; back up single-language puns; fix an official short name early |
| F12 | Template voice 模板腔 | Defaulting to 〜件／這件事 — only 20 published 〜な件 LN titles existed by early 2019 | Reads as imitation, not style | At most once per set, and only when the narration truly is a deadpan report |

## 2. The 12-question gate

Run every check on every title. Record results privately; only the one-line report is shown.

| Gate | Question | Catches | On failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 Swap | Name three real or plausible works in this genre — does the title fit two or more of them without edits? | F1, F7 | Add or replace the anchor |
| G2 Picture | Can the anchor be drawn as a thumbnail? | F7 | Swap abstract for concrete |
| G3 Problem | Is there a handicap, cost, constraint or irony? | F2 | Add a constraint or defect |
| G4 Spoiler | Does it touch anything on the do-not-reveal list? | F3 | Hint (symbol, euphemism, question) instead |
| G5 Promise | Is every promise word the material's core loop, with an evidence quote? | F4 | Replace with the true loop |
| G6 Collision | Too close to a famous title, a trending template filler, or another proposal in this set? | F5 | Change the filler; change the archetype |
| G7 Length | Within the market cap, with a natural break, parseable in one read? | F6 | Cut to 2–4 slots; split main ＋ subtitle |
| G8 Jargon | At most one coined term; genre clear in about a second? | F8 | Replace coined terms with known words |
| G9 Handle | Sayable, short, searchable, not a bare common word? | F6, F11 | Redesign the handle |
| G10 Voice | Matches the work's tone; cliché template used at most once in the set? | F12 | Rewrite ending and punctuation |
| G11 Medium | Does every long-band title — and every mid-band title with cross-media planned — have an elegant／short name? | F9 | Add the dual name |
| G12 Red line ＆ translation | Any red-line word for the target market? Is the anchor translatable if export is planned? | F10, F11 | Remove the word; re-anchor |

## 3. Severity and gate report

- **Hard fails — rewrite or drop:** G1, G4, G5, and the red-line half of G12.
- **Disclosed soft fails count as passed with an exception:** list them under 刻意保留 in the gate report, not as failures.
- **Soft fails — fix, or disclose in the proposal's 風險與對策 line:** G2, G3, G6–G11, and the translation half of G12. A deliberately over-cap title is allowed only with a handle, a short name and a note.
- **Never certify availability.** Recommend a collision search (platform, bookstore, trademark database) instead of claiming a title is unused.
- **Gate report (one line):** checks passed, deliberate exceptions kept, and directions excluded with the reason — e.g. 「已排除：暗示父親失蹤的書名（禁說清單）」.
