# Worked example: grey clay layout → stylized 3D frame

Read this when you want to check clause order, density and the notes that go with a finished prompt. Copy the structure, not the characters, vehicle or setting.

## Input

- **Image 1 (layout)**: a 16:9 grey clay render. Low camera behind and to the left of a small open-bed kei truck that is driving away up a mountain road curving left. A tall steep slope with large diagonal folds fills the right half of the frame, rolling hills sit on the left, and open sky fills the upper left. Two elderly passengers lean over the bed's left side rail just behind the cab, visible only from the chest up. The driver shows only as the back of a head through the cab's rear window.
- **Images 2–5 (references)**: grandfather full body, grandmother full body, the driver's back-of-head close-up, and the truck seen from the rear.
- **Image 6 (style reference)**: a finished frame from the same project: stylized 3D, soft volume, saturated cobalt sky, rounded leaf-cluster trees, a subtle violet-cornered vignette, and no outlines.
- **Brief**: an early-morning mountain road left muddy by a landslide, with fallen trees. The previous shot had settled on lighting with "a little more contrast", and the user asked to render this shot "the same way".

## Delivered 構思 bullets (summary)

- Composition locked to the clay layout: the rear three-quarter low angle, the truck driving away, the road curving left, and the slope and hill silhouettes kept and dressed with canopy and one smooth landslide band.
- Only the visible parts are described: the elders from the chest up and the driver's back of head. Shorts, skirt and sandals are left out so the model doesn't zoom out.
- The grandmother's jade bangle is kept, so the previous shot's negative prompt, which banned bracelets for a different character, was not reused.
- The plate is centered below the tailgate as in the layout, and the small door lettering is suppressed.
